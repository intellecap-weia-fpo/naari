# Privacy notice

This notice describes how NAARI handles personal data. Each organisation that runs NAARI decides why and how personal data is processed, so it is the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act). It must give its users and respondents its own notice and contact details.

## What is collected

Depending on how each survey is set up: a respondent's name, age, household and demographic details, phone number, location, photo, signature and answers. For staff accounts: name, email and phone number.

## Why

Personal data is processed only to run the service: to manage accounts and access, and to carry out the purpose the operating organisation states when it collects the data.

## Consent

The operating organisation obtains consent before collecting personal data and records how it can be withdrawn. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting that organisation.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask the operating organisation to give you a summary of your data, correct or erase it, and name someone to act for you. You can also raise a grievance with it and, if it stays unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India. Send requests to the organisation that collected your data. The software does not yet offer self-service deletion, so its administrators act on these requests.

## Children

NAARI is meant for adults. Organisations must not collect a child's data without the verifiable parental consent the DPDP Act requires.

## Where data goes

Data stays in the database and file storage of the organisation's own server. Optional services listed in the README, such as LLM endpoints and error monitoring, receive data only if the organisation turns them on.

## Retention

Each organisation sets its own retention period and erases personal data when its purpose has been met.

## Applicable laws

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; Information Technology Act 2000; the Reasonable Security Practices and Sensitive Personal Data Rules 2011; the Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code Rules 2021.

## Contact

For questions about this project, write to dpg@invarya.com. For your own data, contact the organisation that collected it.
