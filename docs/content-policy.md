# Content and abuse policy

NAARI is a survey platform for measuring women's empowerment, agency and resilience in agriculture. What users submit is private to the organisation running the deployment, and the software does not publish it.

## What counts as inappropriate or illegal

Content that is illegal under the law of the deployment's country. Also content unrelated to the survey, abusive content, and personal data collected without consent.

## How it is found and handled

- **Finding it:** each organisation's administrators can review its responses, and anyone can report content.
- **Reporting:** report content to the organisation running the deployment. Problems with the software or this project's spaces go to dpg@invarya.com.
- **Removing it:** administrators archive the response, which removes it from reports and exports. Permanent deletion is done on request by the operator of the deployment.
- **Illegal content:** operators remove it and report it where the law requires.
