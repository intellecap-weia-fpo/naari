# Content and abuse policy

NAARI is a survey platform for measuring women's empowerment, agency and resilience in agriculture. Its content is private to the organisation running the deployment: nothing users submit is published by the software.

- **Who moderates.** Each organisation running a deployment owns and moderates its content. Its administrators can review and archive submissions.
- **Reporting.** Anyone can report inappropriate or illegal content to the operator of the deployment. Problems with the software itself, or with this project's own spaces, go to dpg@intellecap.net.
- **Illegal content.** Operators remove it and report it where the law of their country requires.
